Song to Ocarina of Time

Replays any song with the game's own instruments. It all runs in this page, and nothing you add here is uploaded anywhere.

1Your Ocarina of Time ROM

Drop your ROM here (.z64, .v64 or .n64) or click to pick it
The instruments are read out of your own copy of the game.
No instrument names are stored in the ROM, so press ▶ to hear one, then choose which part it plays.

2The song

Drop a song here (mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a or a MIDI file) or click to pick it
A MIDI file is played back note for note. A recording has its notes worked out first.
0.25
0.22lower finds quieter notes
5most notes at once

3Make it

Finding notes in a finished recording is the hard part: clear melodies come out well, while dense mixes give the right tune and harmony rather than a copy. Drums are not picked up. The ROM and the .sf2 made from it are yours alone, so don't share them.
