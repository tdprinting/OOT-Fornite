All Peter audio is original and synthesised by tools/peter/build_peter_audio.py (formant synthesis + babble, numpy only). No Family Guy audio, no recordings of any actor, no voice cloning or imitation of a real person's voice. Cues: greet (laugh), hire ("Alright!" + laugh), attack (grunt + swing), hurt (hiss + yelp), belly (boing + grunt), chicken (grunt flurry), kick ("Hup!" + whoosh), cheer ("Freakin' sweet!"), call (ring, "Hey Peter!", conversation babble timed to dialogue.py subtitles, hang-up). Reproduce: python tools/peter/build_peter_audio.py.
